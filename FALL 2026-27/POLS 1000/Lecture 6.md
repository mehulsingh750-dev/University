- Essay question relates to who controls the power in government, PM, parliament or cabinet
- Prime Minister
	- Characterized as the 'first among equals' with relation to the cabinet
	- Following decisions are made by PM
		- Which members of Parliament will be members of the cabinet
		- Which portfolios will be assigned to which cabinet ministers
		- Which MP's will serve as parliamentary Secretaries
		- Advising the Governor General on dissolution, proroguing the House of Commons (HOC), orders-in-council appointments, the Speech from the Throne.
		- Answers questions during the question period
		- Invoking closure during a debate
- Ministerial Responsibility
	- Cabinet is responsible for its choices and must defend themselves to parliament
	- They are not allowed to publicly disagree with government policy
