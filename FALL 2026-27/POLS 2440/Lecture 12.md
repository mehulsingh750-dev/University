- Powers of the Bureaucrat
	- The Bundesrat has an absolute veto power over some policies
	- Bundesrat is the upper house of the legislature
	- Known as 'Consent bills', includes anything that impacts the competences and budgets of the Lander
	- The Bundestag cannot overrule the Bundesrat on these issues
	- The Bundesrat considers all other bills as well
	- Known as 'Objection Bills', Bundestag can overrule the Bundesrat on these Bills
- Conclusion
	- Germany is a federal system
	- The lander have a constitutionally guaranteed right to exist
	- The Lander vary in terms of geographic size, demographic size and wealth
	- Political powers are divided by the Basic Law between the federal gov and Lander\
	- In some cases, powers are shared between the two
	- The Federal gov has the power to make decisions that affect the entire country
	- But, the Lander are significant in apart because they constitute the upper chamber of the federal legislature 
	- Political comp of Bundesrat does not always align with the political composition of the Bundestag
